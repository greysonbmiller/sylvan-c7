FAQ

Common questions, straight answers

The questions worth settling before a project starts — pricing, process, ownership, and what happens after launch — grouped so you can jump to what matters to you.

Pricing & Payment

How much does a website cost?
Most projects start at $2,500 for a straightforward brochure site, $5,500 for a site built to actively sell, and around $10,000 for custom stores, booking platforms, and complex integrations. Every number is a starting point — you'll get an exact, fixed quote after a free call. See the full breakdown on the Pricing page.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Typically that's a deposit to begin work and a final payment at launch, though larger projects can be split into more milestones. We agree on a payment schedule as part of your quote, so there are no surprises.
Is a care plan required?
No. Care plans are entirely optional and start at $99/month for hosting, backups, and security updates. You're free to host the finished site anywhere and manage it however you like — the site is yours either way.

Process & Timeline

How long does it take?
A Launch-tier site typically takes 2–3 weeks, a Growth-tier site with a CMS and more pages runs 4–6 weeks, and custom or e-commerce builds are scoped individually — typically 8–12 weeks depending on scope. Timelines also depend on how quickly you can turn around content and feedback.
What do you need to start?
A rough idea of what you sell, who you sell it to, and any existing branding, photos, or copy you already have. If your content isn't ready, we can help you get there — but the sooner it's ready, the faster we can move.
What if the design isn't right?
A Launch project includes one round of revisions and a Growth project includes two; on a Commerce & Custom build, revision rounds are scoped with the rest of the project. We show you design direction early, before it's built out fully, so we can adjust course before a lot of work is locked in.
What if there's already a website?
We can rebuild it from scratch or migrate your existing content into a new design — either way, we'll review what you have and tell you honestly what's worth keeping. Migration complexity is one of the things that factors into your quote.
Do you write the copy?
Copywriting support is included with the Growth and Commerce & Custom packages, and can be scoped separately for a Launch project if you need it. You're also welcome to provide your own copy — either way, we'll make sure it reads well and supports your SEO.
Do you do logos or branding?
Not typically — our focus is the website itself, not brand identity. If you don't have a logo or brand colors yet, we can point you toward resources or collaborators, and build the site to accommodate branding once you have it.

Technical & Ownership

Who owns the website?
Yes, completely. That includes the code, the domain, your content, and the accounts behind them — nothing is licensed back to us and nothing disappears if you stop working with us.
Where is it hosted?
Wherever you'd like. We can set you up with hosting as part of a care plan, or hand everything off so you host it yourself with a provider of your choice. There's no requirement to host through us.
Can the site be edited without a developer?
Growth and Commerce & Custom sites include a content management system as standard, so you can update text, images, and pages yourself, and we walk you through how to use it before launch. A Launch-tier site can have a CMS added as a paid option if you want that flexibility.
Will it work on phones?
Yes — your site will be designed mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets, and desktops before it goes live. That's a baseline on every package, not an upgrade.
Will it show up on Google?
Every project includes on-page SEO fundamentals — clean structure, fast load times, proper metadata, and mobile-friendliness — which are the foundation search engines look for. Growth and Commerce packages add more advanced SEO work for businesses that want to actively compete for search traffic.
What about accessibility and ADA compliance?
Accessibility is considered from the start of every project, not bolted on afterward — things like color contrast, keyboard navigation, and semantic markup. It's not a substitute for a full legal accessibility audit if your business needs one, but it puts you on solid footing.

After Launch

What happens after launch?
You get a walkthrough of how to use and update your site, all your account credentials and files, and the option to add ongoing care starting at $99/month if you'd rather someone else handle hosting, backups, and updates. If you'd rather take it from there yourself, that works too.
Do you work with businesses outside Los Angeles?
Yes. Sylvan C7 is based in Los Angeles, California, but works remotely with clients anywhere in the US — calls, feedback, and revisions all happen online, so location isn't a barrier.
What industries can you work with?
We take on projects in any industry. Whatever the field, a project would start the same way — learning how your customers actually find and choose a business like yours, then building the site around that. The specifics differ between a restaurant, a law practice, a home service company, and an online store; the process doesn't.
