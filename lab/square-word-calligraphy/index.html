From the lab

Square Word Calligraphy

Type English and each word comes out as one square block that looks like a Chinese character, the way the artist Xu Bing imagined it in 1994. It runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

I built this solo, as my own experiment: a custom layout engine and renderer, with SVG and PNG export.

How to read a square word

Every letter is there, packed into a square. Read left to right, top to bottom, outside to inside.

Credits

I wrote the software. The idea, the alphabet and the type belong to other people.

  • Xu Bing

    Invented Square Word Calligraphy (英文方块字) in 1994 and taught it through his Square Word Calligraphy Classroom. Read his own account.

  • David B. Kelley

    Revised the alphabet in 2012, replacing eleven letters with more Chinese-looking forms. His version, documented on Omniglot, is the one this tool draws.

  • Fonts: LXGW WenKai and Noto Serif SC

    The brush style uses LXGW WenKai and the print styles use Noto Serif SC, both cut down to the characters the alphabet needs and used under the SIL Open Font License: LXGW WenKai license, Noto Serif SC license.

This is my own experiment, not client work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xu Bing or David Kelley.
