From the lab

Nuestra Finca

A small hillside farm, rebuilt in 3D from 17 phone photos and a few tape-measure numbers. There's a kitchen, a wooden house on posts and the covered bridge between them. Walk it, or fly over the roofs.

Getting around

Press H inside for this list. On a touchscreen: left-thumb stick to move, drag to look, double-tap to jump.

  • W A S DWalk
  • MouseLook around
  • ShiftRun (or fly faster)
  • SpaceJump (or rise, when flying)
  • FSwitch walking and flying
  • CSink down, when flying
  • 1–8Jump to a place: kitchen, bridge, house, patio…
  • LLamps on or off

How I built it

  • From photos to a plan

    No drawings, just photos and measurements. I worked out where each photo was taken from. Then I adjusted the model until most corners and posts sit within about 30 cm of where the photos put them.

  • One table of numbers

    A script generates everything from a single list of dimensions. When the house room turned out to be 4 by 4 meters, the fix was one number and a one-minute rebuild.

  • Light worked out in advance

    Sunlight, lamps and soft shadows are calculated once in Blender and painted onto the surfaces. It runs at around 50–60 frames per second, even on basic laptop graphics.

  • Made to be played

    Walls and railings stop you, stairs and the bridge can be climbed, and the picture adjusts its sharpness to keep motion smooth.

Credits

  • Blender

    The model is built and its lighting calculated in Blender, driven entirely by script.

  • three.js and three-mesh-bvh

    The walkthrough draws the model with three.js and handles collisions with three-mesh-bvh, both under the MIT license.

This is my own experiment, not client work. The model is approximate: sizes come from photos and a few measurements, and the furniture is placed by eye.
