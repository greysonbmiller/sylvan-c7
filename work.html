My product

Bibooks: bilingual audiobooks

Turns any text into a sentence-by-sentence bilingual audiobook with read-along highlighting, in any pair of 49 languages.

I built the Bibooks bilingual audiobook app to make the learning material I couldn't find: stories at my level, read aloud in two languages. I designed it and I run all of it, from the job pipeline to billing. Its public library is free to read and listen to without an account, on the web or with the Bibooks Android app on Google Play.

What it shows

  • A full product shipped solo: web app, payments, 49 interface languages.
  • An audio pipeline that syncs highlighting to speech, word by word, across scripts.
  • Production operations: blue/green deploys, monitored backups, self‑hosted CI, thousands of automated tests.
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Stripe
  • Docker

Process

How a project runs

Five steps. Each one ends with something concrete in your hands.

Discovery call

A free 30-minute call, by phone or video, about what you need, what you already have, and what isn't working. No pitch.

You receiveA written summary of the call, yours to keep whether or not you hire me.

Fixed quote in writing

Scope, timeline and price in writing before any work starts. If the build takes longer than expected, that's my problem, not a surprise line on your invoice.

You receiveA fixed quote that moves only if you change the scope, and only after you approve it in writing.

Design you approve

You see the key pages as designs and revise them with me. Changing a layout at this stage is a conversation; changing it after the site is built is a rebuild. Nothing is coded before sign-off.

You receiveDesigns for desktop and mobile, revised through the rounds your package includes.

Build and review

I build what you approved. You review it at fixed checkpoints on your own phone and laptop.

You receiveA private preview link, with your feedback tracked in writing so nothing gets lost.

Launch, training and handover

I handle the launch, then walk you through everything you'll run yourself.

You receiveEvery account in your name, the source code, a walkthrough and a written handover document.

Ongoing care is an optional add-on, not a sixth step. It's month to month, and ending it stops the maintenance, not the website.

Verify, don't trust

Standards you can check

Claims are cheap. Every website gets these four, each with a way to check it yourself.

  • Semantic, accessible HTML

    Real headings, landmarks, labeled forms and alt text. Usable by keyboard and screen reader.

    Check itRun WAVE or axe on this page.

  • A published speed budget

    Website pages under 500 KB, with LCP under 2.5 s on throttled 4G.

    Check itRun PageSpeed Insights or Lighthouse.

  • No proprietary platform

    Open web standards on hosting bought in your name. No platform fee, and nothing that breaks if you leave.

    Check itAsk me what breaks if you move hosts. The answer is nothing.

  • Code another developer can pick up

    Readable, version-controlled source, structured the way a working developer expects.

    Check itHave any developer you trust read it.
