How It Works

No surprises, start to finish

The parts of hiring a web developer that usually go wrong are the parts we pin down in writing before anything begins.

01

Discovery call

A 30-minute call, by phone or video, about how your business actually gets customers and what the site needs to do. No pitch, no obligation.

02

Fixed quote in writing

You get scope, timeline and a fixed price before any work starts. The number doesn't move unless you change the scope.

03

Design you approve

You see and sign off on the design before a single line of code is written.

04

Build and review

The approved design gets built, with review checkpoints on real phones and laptops so you see it working before it goes live.

05

Launch, training and handover

We go live, walk you through running it yourself, and hand over every account. You own all of it.

That's the whole process — five steps, nothing hidden.

Your Risk, Removed

Three things you can hold
us to in writing.

Hiring a developer means trusting someone before you can see the work. These three terms are how that risk stays on our side of the table.

You approve the design first

Not a line of code is written until you have seen the design and said yes to it. If it isn't right, it changes then — before the expensive part starts.

You own everything from day one

The code, the domain, the hosting and every account are in your name from the start — so any other developer can pick the site up and keep going without asking us for anything.

No lock-in contract

There is no minimum term and no auto-renewing agreement. Ongoing care is optional and month to month; you stay because the work is worth staying for.
