What I build

Six kinds of work, one standard

Websites

Fast, accessible sites for small businesses, built by hand without a proprietary platform and sold as fixed packages.

  • A design you approve before any code
  • Mobile-first, checked on real devices
  • Online stores and booking
  • Redesigns and platform migrations

Web apps

Browser-based tools that replace the spreadsheet, email chain or manual process your team works around.

  • Dashboards and admin panels
  • Customer portals and accounts
  • Logins and permissions for your team
  • Built in phases, working code early

Integrations

Connect the systems you already pay for, so data moves between them without copy and paste.

  • CRM, payments, scheduling and email
  • Scheduled jobs and automated reports
  • APIs and webhooks between services
  • Alerts when something fails

Instrument control

Software that drives physical instruments and has to keep running unattended.

  • Clear screens to run and monitor a system
  • Careful handling of faults and stops
  • A log of every run
  • Tests that run without the hardware

Data tooling

Pipelines, checks and reports for data where a wrong answer is worse than no answer.

  • Import and clean data from files and systems
  • Checks that flag bad data, not pass it on
  • Reports and exports you can trust
  • Repeatable scripts, not one-off spreadsheets

Care plans

Optional monthly care after launch, so your site stays secure, backed up and current.

  • Hosting, SSL and automated backups
  • Uptime monitoring and security updates
  • Content edits and tuning (Active and up)
  • Month to month, cancel anytime

FAQ

Questions before you ask

How is custom software priced?

Scoped per project, with a fixed quote in writing after a free call. Larger builds can be split into paid phases, starting small so you see working code before committing further.

How does payment work?

A deposit books your slot and the balance is due at launch. Larger projects are split into milestones, agreed in the quote.

How long does a project take?

Launch sites typically take 2–3 weeks, Growth sites 4–6 weeks, and Commerce & Custom builds 8–12 weeks. How quickly content and feedback come back affects the timeline too.

What do you need from me to start?

A rough idea of what you offer, who it’s for, and any branding, photos or copy you already have. If content isn’t ready, I can help; the sooner it’s ready, the faster things move.

What if the design isn’t right?

You approve the design before anything is coded. Launch includes one round of revisions and Growth two; for Commerce & Custom and custom software, revision rounds are scoped with the project.

Who owns it, and where is it hosted?

You own everything from day one: code, domain, hosting and accounts, all in your name. Host it anywhere you like. A care plan can cover hosting, but it’s never required.

Can I edit the site without a developer?

Yes. Growth and Commerce & Custom sites include a CMS as standard, and I walk you through it before launch. On a Launch site, a CMS is a paid add-on.

What about accessibility?

Every project gets semantic, accessible HTML with readable contrast and keyboard navigation, which you can check yourself with WAVE or axe. It isn’t a substitute for a legal accessibility audit.

Not sure which fits? Start with a free call.

Thirty minutes, phone or video, no pitch. I reply within two business days.
